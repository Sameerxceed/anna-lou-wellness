You sit in the car on the drive a minute longer than you need to, engine off. And the questions arrive.

  • Am I doing any of this right?
  • Are the kids actually alright, or have they just got good at saying they are?
  • Do I want to leave, or do I need to do the work myself?
  • Do I even know who I am anymore?

Nothing has gone wrong. You are not broken and you are not ungrateful. You have been running the same pattern for a long time, and patterns are quiet. They show up as living for the next holiday. As a low hum of not quite enough underneath everything. As a version of you that everybody recognises except you.

They are also the patterns your children are learning by watching you.

You have tried things, and you were not wrong to. The breathwork. The cold water. The crystals someone swore by. Maybe therapy, or maybe you are in it now. All of it helps. It rarely finishes the job on its own, because the thing holding you in place is not stored in your thinking. It is held in your body, and until it is released there it will keep quietly running the show.

This is not about becoming someone else. It is about getting the signal back online, so you can hear what you actually want. Reset Letters is a weekly blog about exactly that, and a global wellness community of people doing the same thing, in their own lives, all over the world.
